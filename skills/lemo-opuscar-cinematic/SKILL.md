# Lemo Opuscar: Cinematic Code-Authored Film Styles Skill

Lemo Opuscar collects and executes algorithmic film styles, visual directions, and camera languages constructed through code.

## 10 Production Film Styles

1. **Pixel Art 16-Bit RPG**:
   - Canvas pixel scaling (`image-rendering: pixelated; transform: scale(4)`).
   - 16-color restricted palette (e.g. Endesga 32 or Dawnbringer 16).
   - Chiptune audio synchronization with 8-bit sound effects.

2. **Cyberpunk Neon Noir**:
   - Deep obsidian background (#030712) with neon cyan (#00f3ff) and magenta (#ff0055) glows.
   - Scanline overlays, subtle CRT bloom, chromatic aberration shader.
   - Low-frequency synthwave drone and bass pulses.

3. **Minimalist Bauhaus / Swiss Modernism**:
   - Strict asymmetric grid layouts, Helvetica Neue / Inter bold typography.
   - Primary color triad: Cadmium Red (#e11d48), Ultramarine Blue (#2563eb), Mustard Yellow (#eab308).
   - Rhythmic geometric transformations with hard cuts and zero decoration.

4. **Origami & Papercraft 3D**:
   - CSS 3D perspective transforms (`perspective: 1200px; transform-style: preserve-3d`).
   - Dynamic paper folding along hinge lines (`rotateX`, `rotateY`).
   - Ambient drop-shadows with simulated paper creases.

5. **Blueprint / Technical Architectural Draft**:
   - Cyan drafting grid (#0b3866 background, #1e5ba3 grid lines).
   - Crisp white / neon cyan vector strokes with architectural dimension callouts.
   - Orthographic and isometric projections rotating on technical axes.

6. **Cel Anime Action**:
   - High-contrast cel shading with dramatic rim lighting.
   - Radial speed lines, impact frames, camera shake on hits.
   - Dynamic perspective tilts (Dutch angles) and rapid zooms.

7. **Dark Victorian / Gothic Silhouette**:
   - Rich pitch-black vector silhouettes against atmospheric twilight fog.
   - Volumetric streetlamp cones, glowing windows, lantern halos.
   - Ambient contemplative drone with delicate clock chimes.

8. **Claymation / Stop-Motion Miniature**:
   - Stepped frame rate (10-12 fps) with tactile finger-press surface imperfections.
   - Warm studio key-and-fill lighting with shallow depth-of-field blur.

9. **Vaporwave Surrealism (90s CGI)**:
   - Wireframe neon grids receding into an infinite sunset horizon.
   - Pastel lavender and mint palettes, spinning wireframe primitives, marble pillars.

10. **Kinetic Infographic Documentary**:
    - High-density network nodes, animated bezier connection trees, real-time counters.
    - Precision HUD telemetry, coordinate readouts, sleek corporate tech aesthetics.
