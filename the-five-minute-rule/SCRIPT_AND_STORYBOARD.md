# THE 5-MINUTE RULE (An Animated Short Film)
**Format:** 2D Minimalist Silhouette Vector Animation  
**Style Reference:** *Last Soul ("1 MINUTE")*, *Pursuit of Wonder*, *Sisyphus 55*  
**Resolution:** 1920x1080 (16:9 Landscape) @ 30 FPS  
**Target Duration:** 5 Minutes (300 Seconds)  

---

## 1. Synopsis & Core Message
A person trapped in a perpetual cycle of snooze buttons and burnout tries to fix their entire life with massive, impossible habits. Paralyzed by the scale of what they think they must become, they give up before even tying their shoes. But a simple, quiet conversation reframes everything: *What if you didn't have to change your whole life today? What if you just gave it five minutes?*

---

## 2. Visual Aesthetic Guidelines
- **Character Design:**
  - Solid black silhouettes with crisp vector geometry.
  - Expressive oval white eyes with subtle blinks and emotional tilt.
  - Subtle golden/ambient rim-light on edges when illuminated by streetlamps or the rising sun.
  - Glowing minimalist accessories (e.g. smartwatch screen glowing neon cyan/blue on the wrist).
- **Environment Design:**
  - Layered flat/semi-flat vector scenery with depth gradients.
  - Cinematic lighting: Streetlamp volumetric cone halos, window blinds cast shadows, warm store interiors vs. cold twilight outdoors.
  - Deep blue/charcoal palette (#0b101d, #141c2e, #1a233a) evolving gradually into warm sunrise hues (#f39c12, #e74c3c, #f1c40f) by Scene 5.
- **Cinematography & Pacing:**
  - Slow, contemplative camera pans, slow zooms (punch-ins for emotional beats), and parallax movement.
  - Dialogue appears as introspective on-screen text with a soft gaussian dark smoke backing, allowing the viewer to absorb the emotion at their own pace.

## 3. Five-Scene Structure (60 Seconds Per Scene)

### **Scene 1: The Snooze & The Inertia (0:00 – 1:00 / 0s – 60s)**
* **Setting:** Pitch-black bedroom at 5:30 AM.
* **Key Visuals:**
  - Close-up on smartphone screen glowing in the dark: "5:30 AM — RUN 5 KM".
  - White rounded "SNOOZE" button pulsates softly.
  - Protagonist's silhouette hand slowly enters frame, hesitates, and presses SNOOZE.
  - On-screen thought: *"It's just five minutes..."*
  - Fast-forward montage: clock spinning through days, weeks, months. The feeling of being stuck.
* **Narration:**
  > "Every morning begins with a negotiation with yourself. You promise that tomorrow will be different. But when 5:30 AM arrives, the mountain feels too steep to climb, so you choose the comfort of the pillow. And the days slip through your fingers."

---

### **Scene 2: The Unbearable Mountain (1:00 – 2:00 / 60s – 120s)**
* **Setting:** Dimly lit study / desk under a single hanging lightbulb.
* **Key Visuals:**
  - A wall-sized calendar filled with aggressive red crosses marking failed days.
  - A notepad with impossible rules: "10 KM EVERY DAY", "WAKE UP AT 4:30 AM", "NO DISTRACTIONS".
  - Protagonist sits slumped in a chair, face buried in their hands. The weight of unrealistic expectations.
  - On-screen thought: *"Why is it so impossible to start?"*
* **Narration:**
  > "We don't fail because we are lazy. We fail because our ambition is violent. We expect ourselves to cross the entire ocean in a single jump. And because the jump is impossible... we never even take the first step."

---

### **Scene 3: The Encounter (2:00 – 3:00 / 120s – 180s)**
* **Setting:** The shoe store / park bench (homage to *Last Soul*).
* **Key Visuals:**
  - Minimalist modern store counter, shelves lined with pristine running shoes.
  - A friendly silhouette figure (the store clerk / mentor) behind the counter waving gently: *"Hi. It's been a while."*
  - Protagonist hands over brand new, untouched running shoes to return them: *"I couldn't do it. I haven't run a single day."*
  - The mentor slides the shoes back across the counter and asks one quiet question: *"Can you just put them on and walk outside for five minutes?"*
* **Narration:**
  > "You don't need to conquer the marathon today. Five minutes won't make you an athlete, but it will do something far more important: it will shatter the paralysis of zero."

---

### **Scene 4: The First Stride (3:00 – 4:00 / 180s – 240s)**
* **Setting:** The winding park pathway lined with bare winter trees and glowing vintage streetlamps (homage to screenshot 2).
* **Key Visuals:**
  - One-point perspective curving asphalt road disappearing into the twilight fog.
  - Protagonist in running gear, white eyes alert, smartwatch glowing softly.
  - Character walks with deliberate, calm steps along the road.
  - Warm pools of light illuminate the ground as they pass under each lantern, casting dynamic elongated shadows behind them.
  - A floating minimalist digital stopwatch: `01:00` ... `02:30` ... `04:15` ... `04:59`.
* **Narration:**
  > "The first sixty seconds feel heavy. Your mind invents ten reasons to turn back. But as the cool air fills your chest and your shoes hit the pavement, something strange happens. The resistance fades. You aren't fighting the mountain anymore. You're just taking the next step."

---

### **Scene 5: The Dawn of Momentum (4:00 – 5:00 / 240s – 300s)**
* **Setting:** Park bench overlooking the morning horizon.
* **Key Visuals:**
  - Stopwatch hits `05:00` with a gentle chime.
  - Protagonist pauses, standing tall by the bench, looking out at the sky.
  - Deep midnight blue gradients shift into radiant amber, rose, and gold dawn sunlight.
  - Warm golden rim light bathes the silhouette.
  - Character pulls out a small pocket notebook and marks one crisp green tick: *"Day 1: 5 Minutes."*
  - Camera cranes slowly upward toward the vast open morning sky.
  - Final Title Reveal: **THE 5-MINUTE RULE** / *"The journey begins with five minutes."*
* **Narration:**
  > "A mountain is not moved all at once; it is moved stone by stone. You don't need endless motivation to change your life. You only need the courage to give yourself five minutes. Tomorrow is waiting."
