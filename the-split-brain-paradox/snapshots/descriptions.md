# Film Snapshots & Scene Descriptions

### Frame 01 (t = 15.0s) — Scene 1: The Illusion of the Singular Self
- **Setting:** Deep obsidian twilight void.
- **Visuals:** The human silhouette in profile with glowing cyan (Left Brain / Language & Logic) and amber (Right Brain / Spatial & Intuition) hemispheres connected by the radiant corpus callosum bridge.
- **Beat:** "You believe you are a single mind. But that is an illusion maintained by a bridge."

### Frame 02 (t = 82.0s) — Scene 2: The Severed Bridge & The Alien Hand
- **Setting:** Surgical blueprint with red cut indicator and decoupled cerebral hemispheres.
- **Visuals:** Left and right hemispheres pulled apart. Two hands in conflict over a button-up shirt—the right hand buttons, while the alien left hand relentlessly unbuttons.
- **Beat:** "Two distinct wills living inside the same body."

### Frame 03 (t = 146.0s) — Scene 3: The Experiment of Two Worlds
- **Setting:** Sperry & Gazzaniga's Caltech tachistoscope chamber with red central fixation cross.
- **Visuals:** The partition dividing visual fields. The right visual field sees "CHICKEN CLAW" (left brain), while the left visual field sees "SNOW" (right brain). The left hand reaches out and selects the snow shovel.
- **Beat:** "The speaking left hemisphere saw the claw. The silent right hemisphere saw the snow."

### Frame 04 (t = 205.0s) — Scene 4: The Great Confabulator (The Interpreter Module)
- **Setting:** Internal theater of cognition with spinning cybernetic clockwork gears.
- **Visuals:** The left hemisphere's instant retrospective rationalization: "Oh, that's easy! You need a shovel to clean out the chicken coop."
- **Beat:** "The left brain instantly invented a fiction, believed its own lie, and defended it as reality."

### Frame 05 (t = 275.0s) — Scene 5: The Symphony of the Modular Mind
- **Setting:** Expanding dawn horizon with constellation nodes of the modular parliament.
- **Visuals:** The brain dissolves into decentralized specialized networks orbiting the brilliant illuminated Interpreter module, culminating in the golden title reveal.
- **Beat:** "Ask yourself: is your explanation the truth... or is it just your Interpreter telling you a story?"
